Pawprint privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Pawprint is an offline-first iPhone app designed to help you track dogs, routines and health records. The app does not require an account and does not transmit your stored information to our servers.

Information you enter

Dog profiles, photographs, routines, treatment notes, sitter care details and bookmarked guides are stored on your device using Apple's on-device storage. Pawprint has no developer-operated backend, advertising, analytics, tracking or cloud sync.

Optional system features

When you enable a routine reminder, iOS may ask for notification permission. If denied, the routine can still be used without notifications. Choosing a dog photograph uses Apple's photo picker, without requesting broad photo-library access. If you create a sitter handover, the selected content leaves the device only when you invoke the system share sheet and choose a destination. Handling by that destination is governed by its own terms.

Storage, access and deletion

The app's widget may read a limited local summary of upcoming tasks and tips shared within Apple's App Group storage; it does not receive private sitter notes. You can remove individual records or dog profiles within the app. Deleting Pawprint may remove all locally stored data; there is no server backup or account recovery.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. For enquiries, visit our support page.
