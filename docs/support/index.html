Pawprint support

Pawprint stores your data on your iPhone and works without internet access. For issues with a reminder, check iOS Settings → Notifications → Pawprint. For widgets, make sure you've added a dog and a routine in the app.

For bug reports and questions, use the Pawprint issue tracker, if enabled by the repository owner. Do not include contact details, medical notes or private care-sheet contents in public issues.
